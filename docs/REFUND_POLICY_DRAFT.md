# Refund Policy Draft

Date: 2026-08-05

Status: product draft, not legal advice. Review before public launch.

## Suggested Founder/Beta Refund Position

Offer a simple refund window for early paid users, for example 14 days from purchase, if the app does not meet the user's needs.

## Important Product Limitation

Refund copy should clearly state that source support is best-effort and may vary by website, region, account access, DRM, platform changes, and local environment. The product must not promise that every site or every specific protected source will work.

## Recommended Refund Conditions

Refunds should be available when:

- the app cannot run on a supported OS after basic troubleshooting;
- core advertised features fail for the user's ordinary, supported workflow;
- the user bought the wrong plan and requests help promptly.

Refunds may be denied when:

- the request depends on unsupported DRM/CAPTCHA/paywall/login bypass;
- the user expected guaranteed support for a source that was not promised;
- the refund window has passed;
- abuse/fraud is suspected.

## Support Before Refund

Offer a safe diagnostic path before refund when practical. Diagnostics must avoid cookies, tokens, transcripts, and private paths by default.
