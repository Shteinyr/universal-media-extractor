Known limitations

Best-effort source support.

Universal Media Extractor uses local media engines such as yt-dlp, ffmpeg, and Whisper. Websites can change without notice, and a source that worked before may stop working.

Not supported

  • DRM bypass.
  • CAPTCHA bypass.
  • Paywall bypass.
  • Login restriction bypass.
  • Unauthorized access or downloads.

Performance

Large files can take a long time to download, merge, transcribe, and store. Whisper quality depends on audio quality and selected model.
