Privacy draft

Local-first by design.

Universal Media Extractor is designed to process media locally on your computer. The current product does not require uploading source media, transcripts, cookies, passwords, or API keys to a cloud service.

Data processed locally

  • URLs or local files you provide.
  • Media metadata, outputs, transcripts, logs, and diagnostics.
  • Local output folder paths and job history.

Credentials

The public product should not store passwords, cookies, tokens, or DRM keys. Diagnostics should be redacted before sharing.
