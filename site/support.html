Help without exposing private data.

Support focuses on installation, local app behavior, diagnostics, output folders, and best-effort media engine compatibility.

Safe to share

  • App version and OS version.
  • Normalized error category.
  • Redacted diagnostics bundle.

Do not share

  • Passwords, cookies, tokens, private URLs, transcripts with sensitive content, or private local paths.
